## Cross-page audit conclusions

- Sections were added; no existing route, redirect, canonical, robots, hreflang, or locale architecture was removed or changed.
- No outbound scientific citation was added because the Wave 1 scope is commercial sourcing and first-party evidence, not a research review.
- Source-specific COA material remains batch-specific and is paired with current-document language.
- The soy page does not claim soy PS 50% availability without soy-specific evidence.
- The comparison page owns source-selection intent; the PS 50% page owns concentration/grade intent; the supplier page owns qualification intent.
