OEM and Private-Label Phosphatidylserine Program

Run a private-label PS program from specification to retail-ready packaging: grade selection, sample approval, artwork coordination and documented production handoff.

Discuss This Program View PS Grades

The problem this program addresses

Brands and OEM buyers lose weeks when the ingredient specification, sample approval, packaging and document package are not aligned before production. A structured program removes that ambiguity.

Decisions this program helps you make

  • Which grade, source and specification match the target product?
  • What sample approval sequence is required before production?
  • Who owns artwork, packaging and retail format decisions?
  • Which documents must accompany the finished product?

How the program runs

Five steps from first brief to repeat orders. Each step confirms documents before the next one starts.

01

Program brief

Share target format, market, annual volume, packaging concept and document needs.

02

Grade and spec confirmation

Lock the controlled specification and confirm packaging compatibility before sampling.

03

Sample approval

Approve the pre-production sample against the finished-product brief.

04

Artwork and packaging

Coordinate print, packaging format and retail requirements.

05

Production and handoff

Confirm batch documentation, packaging and shipment planning.

Scope and evidence limits

MOQ, lead time and packaging options are confirmed per program. Certificate coverage is product-specific and must be verified for the quoted construction.

Evidence: Quality & R&D · Manufacturing · Company verification
