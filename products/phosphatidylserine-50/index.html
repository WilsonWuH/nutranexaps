Frequently Asked Questions

What is phosphatidylserine 50% used for?

PS 50% is a concentration-focused phosphatidylserine grade used in dietary supplements and functional nutrition formats such as capsules, tablets, and powder blends. Application fit should be confirmed through your formulation, processing, and stability review.

Is your PS50 from soy or sunflower?

Both source routes are available. Confirm the source for your quotation and request the source statement with the current batch documentation before approval.

What is the typical lead time and MOQ?

The standard MOQ is 25 kg — one 25 kg net drum. Production and delivery lead times are confirmed with the quotation for your destination market and order volume.

Which documents can you provide?

Buyers can request the current specification, batch COA, TDS, SDS, allergen information, and Halal or Kosher certificates. Documents are always matched to the quoted product, source, and batch.

Do you offer non-GMO PS50?

GMO statements are available for the quoted product. Confirm the current non-GMO documentation options for your source route and destination market with our team.

Can you support custom specifications?

Discuss your target assay, source, packaging, and application with our technical support team. Custom requirements are confirmed against production and QC capability before quotation.

Grade decision guide

How buyers should evaluate PS 50%

PS 50% describes the target phosphatidylserine concentration; source, carrier, test method, and current product documents still determine whether a quoted material fits the project.

What PS 50% means

PS 50% is a concentration or grade reference, not a source claim. Confirm whether the quoted material is soy-derived, sunflower-derived, or another documented route, then match the source and grade to the label and market brief.

How PS content is tested

Use the assay method and acceptance limit stated in the current controlled specification or batch COA. Request the test basis, lot number, report date, and any external laboratory reference before qualification.

Application fit

PS 50% may be considered for capsules, tablets, powders, and functional nutrition concepts where the target serving, carrier, flow, moisture, oxidation control, and stability plan have been tested.

Documentation to request

Request the current specification, batch-specific COA, source statement, allergen information, TDS, MSDS, packaging, MOQ, and market-specific files where applicable.
