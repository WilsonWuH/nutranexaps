What Was Reported

FDA's dietary supplement compliance program describes areas used in regulatory oversight, including product identity, statement of identity, ingredient and label requirements, claims, manufacturing controls, and enforcement pathways.

A compliance program is not a finding against a specific PS supplier. It is a useful reference for understanding why a finished-product or importer file should connect labels, ingredients, manufacturing, testing, and records.

Ingredient Buyer Context

US PS buyers should keep the product identity and supplier specification aligned with the finished-product master record, label, COA, and change-control file. Ask the supplier for current documents, but retain an internal review showing how the ingredient fits the finished product.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.

Nutranexa Takeaway

The program does not create a new PS specification or guarantee. It is reported as a quality-system reference.

For a product-specific discussion, provide the target market, application, source preference, PS grade, annual quantity, packaging, and required documents. Nutranexa can then discuss the relevant product and quality evidence without assuming facts that are not in the current controlled file.
