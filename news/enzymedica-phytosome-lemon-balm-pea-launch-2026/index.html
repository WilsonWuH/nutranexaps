Enzymedica Expands Phytosome Line With Lemon Balm and PEA Formulas

By Nutranexa News | August 20, 2026

Enzymedica launched Sleep & Stress Lemon Balm Phytosome and Joint & Muscle PEA Phytosome, extending its phospholipid-complex Phytosome collection to relaxation and joint-support formats.

What Launched

Enzymedica, a Florida-based supplement maker, announced on August 19, 2026 the launch of two additions to its Phytosome collection: Sleep & Stress Lemon Balm Phytosome, a melatonin-free natural sleep aid with a suggested retail price of $24.99 for 60 count, and Joint & Muscle PEA Phytosome at $24.99 for 45 count, which the company describes as its best joint-support supplement.

The two products join the brand's Berberine Phytosome as part of a growing line built around phospholipid-based delivery technology.

Delivery Angle

According to the company, Phytosome technology pairs botanical compounds with phospholipids to improve absorption and utilization of key ingredients. Enzymedica cites clinical findings in its announcement, including participants in a lemon balm phytosome study reporting longer sleep, faster sleep onset, and fewer nighttime awakenings versus placebo, and a six-fold absorption advantage for PEA Phytosome relative to standard micronized PEA.

These figures are company-reported and are summarized here only to describe the launch positioning.

Buyer Takeaway

Phospholipid-complex delivery formats continue to appear in finished supplement launches across categories. Suppliers of lecithin and phospholipid grades can expect brands using complexing technologies to ask about phospholipid ratios, carrier compatibility, stability, and documentation that supports the finished product's quality story.

Nutranexa reports the launch as market news and does not verify the company's clinical or absorption claims.
