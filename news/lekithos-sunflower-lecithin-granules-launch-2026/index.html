Lekithos Launches Sunflower Lecithin Granules for Dry-Format Use

By Nutranexa News | August 1, 2026

Lekithos introduced a sunflower lecithin granule format aimed at dry-format use, adding another non-soy phospholipid option to the supplement and functional-food market.

New Format

Lekithos said it has launched sunflower lecithin granules, adding a dry granulated option alongside more familiar liquid, powder, and capsule formats.

The company presented the product as a non-soy format intended for easier measuring, storage, and use in smoothies, cereals, yogurt, and other routine applications.

Declared Phospholipid Profile

The announcement listed phosphatidylcholine, phosphatidylinositol, and phosphatidylethanolamine amounts per serving, highlighting the product's phospholipid content rather than only a generic lecithin label.

Lekithos also emphasized a single-ingredient, non-GMO, glyphosate-tested positioning for the granule format.

Buyer Takeaway

For buyers, the practical significance is format diversification: dry granules can open different blending and handling workflows than liquid lecithin systems.

Before adoption, ingredient teams will still want full specification, particle-size, flowability, oxidation, and destination-market document support rather than relying on launch copy alone.
