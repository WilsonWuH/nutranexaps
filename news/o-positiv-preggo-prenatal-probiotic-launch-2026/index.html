O Positiv Adds Choline and Probiotics in PREGGO Prenatal Launch

By Nutranexa News | August 1, 2026

O Positiv launched its first prenatal supplement, combining choline, omegas, iron, probiotics, ginger, and vitamin B6 in a single daily formula.

Launch Context

O Positiv's corrected June 10, 2026 release restated a February 10, 2026 launch for PREGGO Prenatal + Probiotic, described as the brand's first prenatal product.

The company positioned the formula around both fetal-development support and common pregnancy comfort concerns such as digestive changes and occasional morning sickness.

Formula Focus

According to the company, the formula combines methylfolate, choline, omegas, iron, probiotics, ginger, and vitamin B6.

The release also emphasized a daily-use prenatal approach extending from preconception through postpartum, rather than a single-stage pregnancy SKU.

Buyer Takeaway

For choline and phospholipid-adjacent suppliers, the launch shows how prenatal brands are bundling core neurodevelopment nutrients with digestive and microbiome support in one line item.

That raises the technical bar on compatibility, capsule load, and substantiation packages when multiple active systems need to coexist in one formula.
