What Was Reported

The PubMed record describes a double-blind randomized controlled trial of crackers enriched with polyunsaturated fatty acids in subjects with overweight. The study was published in Clin Nutr ESPEN in 2026 and registered on ClinicalTrials.gov.

A finished-food trial has a different evidence question from a bulk phospholipid specification. Its results should be read with the actual food matrix, intervention, comparator, duration, population, and endpoints in view.

Ingredient Buyer Context

Functional-food teams evaluating PS or other phospholipids should keep pilot formulation, analytical specification, stability, sensory, and human evidence tracks separate. A trial with a food matrix does not prove that a powder will perform identically in a capsule, beverage, or blend.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.
