What Was Reported

EFSA's novel-food topic page lists a scientific opinion on the safety of roasted seeds from Plukenetia volubilis L. as a traditional food from a third country, published July 24, 2026.

The traditional-food route has its own identity and safe-use evidence questions. It is not a shortcut that can be applied to every plant or phospholipid ingredient with a similar commercial story.

Ingredient Buyer Context

For a PS project, use the exact source and production route in the regulatory file. If a customer asks for traditional-food or novel-food evidence, do not substitute a general food-use statement, a raw-material history, or a different supplier's dossier.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.

Nutranexa Takeaway

The opinion concerns Plukenetia seeds, not phosphatidylserine. Nutranexa reports it as a current EU regulatory-science signal only.
