Real-World Citicoline Study Links Oral Combination to Multidomain Gains

By Nutranexa News | August 1, 2026

A two-center observational cohort associated an oral citicoline combination with improvements in executive, memory, and mood measures in prodromal dementia and early Alzheimer-type decline.

Study Population

The retrospective real-world study followed 100 patients receiving a combined oral citicoline preparation and compared them with 50 age-matched healthy controls.

Participants were assessed at baseline and over a 6-9 month observation period using a broad neuropsychological battery covering executive function, attention, memory, fluency, global cognition, and mood.

Reported Outcomes

The authors reported improvements across several cognitive domains, including executive function, processing speed, working memory, visual-spatial memory, and fluency, together with more favorable mood outcomes.

They also said participants with lower educational levels showed comparatively greater gains, while stressing that the observational design means the findings should be treated as exploratory.

Buyer Takeaway

For citicoline suppliers, the study adds real-world clinical context beyond short acute-performance trials and product-launch messaging.

Because the intervention was a combined preparation rather than isolated citicoline alone, buyers should keep attribution limits clear when using the paper in technical discussions.
