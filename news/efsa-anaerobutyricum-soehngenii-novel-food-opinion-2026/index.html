What Was Reported

EFSA lists a scientific opinion on the safety of Anaerobutyricum soehngenii CH106 as a novel food pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2015/2283, published July 28, 2026. The item is one of several recent novel-food opinions shown on EFSA's topic page.

A novel-food opinion is specific to the organism, product identity, production method, proposed use, and dossier. It does not create a general approval for unrelated ingredients or methods.

Ingredient Buyer Context

When a PS buyer sees a novel-food reference in a customer questionnaire, record the exact route and intended use. Keep the supplier's source and process description consistent with the regulatory assessment and ask a qualified reviewer to confirm whether the reference is relevant.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.
