What Was Reported

The Nestle Nutrition Institute's publication discusses milk fat globule membrane components including phospholipids, sphingomyelin, and gangliosides in the context of early brain development and function. It is an educational review resource, not a Nutranexa product study.

MFGM is a dairy-derived system with its own composition, processing, and evidence questions. It should not be treated as interchangeable with soy or sunflower phosphatidylserine.

Ingredient Buyer Context

The useful sourcing lesson is to define the phospholipid identity and matrix before comparing suppliers. For PS, request source, grade, specification, COA, packaging, and application information rather than using a broad term such as membrane lipid or phospholipid.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.

The publication is reported for ingredient-science context and does not support infant, cognitive, or medical claims for Nutranexa PS.
