What Was Reported

EFSA's emerging-risks page explains that early identification of newly identified or increasing hazards supports operational readiness. It lists drivers including globalisation, international trade, climate change, scientific discovery, resource scarcity, supply-chain concentration, price volatility, and changing diets.

Horizon scanning is not a finding that a specific PS lot is unsafe. It is a way to decide which signals should trigger a supplier question, a change-control review, targeted testing, or a broader risk assessment.

Ingredient Buyer Context

Quality teams can add a short horizon-scanning step to annual supplier review: monitor official alerts, relevant scientific publications, route changes, raw-material signals, and customer complaints. Record why a signal did or did not change the PS testing or qualification plan.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.

Nutranexa Takeaway

Nutranexa reports EFSA's framework as a quality-management reference, not as a PS-specific warning. Buyers should use current, product-specific evidence for release decisions.

For a product-specific discussion, provide the target market, application, source preference, PS grade, annual quantity, packaging, and required documents. Nutranexa can then discuss the relevant product and quality evidence without assuming facts that are not in the current controlled file.
