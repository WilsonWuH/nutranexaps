What Was Reported

NutraIngredients reported that Natural product manufacturer NOW tested 25 vitamin C gummy supplements sold on Amazon and said 13 met the label claim while 12 had nearly no detectable vitamin C. The report is about finished products purchased through a marketplace, not bulk phosphatidylserine.

The story illustrates why a label statement, a supplier COA, and a finished-product result answer different questions. Finished-product dosage form, processing, storage, sampling, and laboratory method can all affect the result seen by a brand or purchaser.

Ingredient Buyer Context

PS buyers can apply the same lesson upstream: define the component specification, check the supplier's test method and sample identity, and ensure the finished-product manufacturer has its own in-process and release controls. A component document should not be stretched into proof of finished-product performance or label accuracy.

The source concerns the named study, company, agency, or market development. It should not be expanded into a medical treatment claim, a universal ingredient result, or a guarantee about a supplier or finished product.
