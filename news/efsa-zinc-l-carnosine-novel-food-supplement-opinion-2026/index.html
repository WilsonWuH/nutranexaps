What Was Reported

EFSA's food and novel-food pages list a scientific opinion on the safety of zinc L-carnosine as a novel food and the bioavailability of zinc from that source in the context of Directive 2002/46/EC, published June 17, 2026.

The listing is specific to a zinc source and its intended supplement context. It should not be read as a general opinion on all food ingredients or phospholipids.

Ingredient Buyer Context

The sourcing lesson is to connect the ingredient identity, source form, proposed use, and regulatory route. A PS buyer should maintain the same discipline when reviewing a customer request for a source statement, food-supplement status, or market-entry document.
