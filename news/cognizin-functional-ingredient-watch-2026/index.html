By Nutranexa News | August 1, 2026

Why It Was Highlighted

Kyowa Hakko USA said market-intelligence firms Mintel and Black Swan Data named Cognizin citicoline a functional ingredient to watch in 2026.

The company framed the recognition as third-party validation that citicoline is moving deeper into mainstream brain-health and mental-performance formulation work.

Format and Region Signal

According to Kyowa's summary of the analysis, 65% of global product launches featuring Cognizin carry a brain or nervous-system claim.

The same summary said launches are concentrated in North America and Europe, while the leading formats are nutrition drinks, energy drinks, and supplements.

Buyer Takeaway

For formulators, the interesting point is not only ingredient visibility but the spread into ready-to-drink and functional-confection formats that need different processing and stability work.

Because the underlying market analysis was summarized by the ingredient owner, buyers should still validate the numbers directly when using them in internal category planning.
