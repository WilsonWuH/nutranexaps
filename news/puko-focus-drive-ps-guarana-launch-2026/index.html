PUKO Nutrition Launches PS and Guarana Nootropic With Disclosed Doses

By Nutranexa News | August 20, 2026

U.S. direct-to-consumer brand PUKO Nutrition launched Focus + Drive, a two-capsule nootropic pairing 200 mg guarana extract with 60 mg phosphatidylserine at fully disclosed doses.

What Launched

PUKO Nutrition, a U.S. direct-to-consumer supplement brand, announced the nationwide launch of PUKO Focus + Drive, described as a daily nootropic for daytime focus. Each two-capsule serving contains 200 mg of guarana extract and 60 mg of phosphatidylserine, with both doses printed on the label and no proprietary blends or synthetic caffeine.

The launch is the first stage of a three-part system that also includes two melatonin-free night formulas using ingredients such as magnesium glycinate, KSM-66 ashwagandha, lemon balm, Montmorency tart cherry, and saffron.

Positioning

The company positions guarana as supporting alertness and reaction time within 30 to 60 minutes, while phosphatidylserine is described as building cognitive support over four to six weeks of consistent daily use.

PUKO said all formulas are third-party tested and non-habit forming, and the products are sold through pukonutrition.com.

Buyer Takeaway

Fully disclosed doses and no-proprietary-blend labels are a visible formulation trend in cognitive supplements. Brands taking this approach typically need suppliers to provide precise assay data and finished-label dose statements for ingredients like PS.

Company launch claims are reported here as the brand's own positioning, not as Nutranexa-endorsed outcomes.
