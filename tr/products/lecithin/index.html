Ürün sayfasını görüntüle

Powdered

Powdered soybean lecithin

PLF powder is the best-documented route in the current material. The supplied sheet describes high acetone-insoluble content and a 24-month shelf-life statement; the current controlled specification must govern the quotation.

Liquid

Liquid soybean lecithin

Liquid grades are described as amber to brown-yellow liquids for emulsification and dispersion support in bakery, chocolate, confectionery, and related food systems.

Granulated

Granulated soybean lecithin

Granulated material is described as a dispersible form for food premixes and applications where powder handling and reduced caking are useful.
