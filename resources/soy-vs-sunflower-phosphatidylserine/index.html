Soy vs Sunflower Phosphatidylserine: B2B Sourcing Guide

B2B sourcing guide comparing soy and sunflower phosphatidylserine by source, grade, allergen documents, formulation fit, and buyer qualification needs.

View PS ProductsRequest Specifications

Soy phosphatidylserine and sunflower phosphatidylserine are source-specific PS options. The right route depends on formulation requirements, brand positioning, destination-market review, and the technical documents available for the exact quoted grade.

What is soy-derived PS?

Soy PS is commonly evaluated when a buyer accepts a soy-derived source route and wants to compare source, grade, and document fit for capsules, tablets, powders, dairy formulas, or functional foods. The ingredient name alone does not establish an allergen conclusion or a specific PS concentration. Request the current signed specification, source statement, allergen information, and batch COA.

Review the soy phosphatidylserine product page for the current source description and document-request path.

What is sunflower-derived PS?

Sunflower PS is a source-specific option for buyers who prefer sunflower-origin positioning or need to keep soy and sunflower routes separate during qualification. The site provides PS 20% and PS 50% sunflower sample COAs for preliminary review. Final availability, grade, carrier, and market documents must be confirmed before purchase.

Review the sunflower phosphatidylserine product page and the PS 50% grade page when comparing source and concentration.

Allergen considerations

Soy and sunflower source names are not substitutes for a current allergen statement. Request source, GMO, processing-aid, and destination-market documents for the exact quoted grade, and have the buyer's regulatory team review final label wording.

Formulation considerations

Compare the intended format, target serving, carrier, flow, moisture exposure, oxidation control, and stability plan. Run application and finished-product testing before treating either source as interchangeable.

Specification comparison

Use the PS powder specifications guide to organize assay, appearance, moisture, peroxide value, insoluble matter, microbiology, storage, and test-method questions. The current source-specific specification and batch COA remain controlling.

Documentation buyers should request

  • Current product specification tied to the quoted source and grade
  • Batch-specific COA and the test basis used for assay and quality indicators
  • Source, allergen, and GMO statements where required
  • Packaging, storage, MOQ, lead time, and sample information
  • Halal, Kosher, or other market-specific files when applicable

Which source should you choose?

Choose soy PS when the project accepts a soy-derived route and the current source and allergen documents fit the target market. Choose sunflower PS when a non-soy position or sunflower-origin route is important and the current specification and source statement support that decision. In either case, qualify the exact product, not only the ingredient name.

When soy PS makes more sense

Soy may be the better starting route when the project accepts soy lecithin-derived material, the buyer already has a qualified soy label position, and the current source-specific documents meet the destination-market review.

When sunflower PS makes more sense

Sunflower may be the better starting route when a non-soy or sunflower-origin position matters and the quoted grade, current source statement, allergen review, and batch evidence support that choice.
